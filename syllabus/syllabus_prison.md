## II. Detailed Syllabus

### 1. General Knowledge (40 Marks)

#### **History (5 Marks)**
* **Kerala:** Arrival of Europeans and their contributions. History of Travancore from Marthanda Varma to Sree Chithira Thirunal. Social, religious, and Renaissance movements. National movements in Kerala and literary sources of Kerala history. Unified Kerala movement. Socio-political history of Kerala after 1956.
* **India:** Political history and British dominance. Freedom struggle and formation of Indian National Congress. Swadeshi movement and social reform movements. Newspapers, art, and literature of the freedom struggle. Mahatma Gandhi and the post-independence era. Reorganization of states. Progress in science, education, and technology, and foreign policy.
* **World:** Great Revolution in England. American War of Independence, French Revolution, Russian Revolution, and Chinese Revolution. Post-WWII political history. United Nations and other international organizations.

#### **Geography (5 Marks)**
* **Basics:** Fundamental principles of geography and earth's structure. Atmosphere, rocks, and earth's surface. Atmospheric pressure, wind, temperature, and seasons. Global warming and pollution types. Topographic maps, symbols, remote sensing, and GIS. Oceans, ocean currents, continents, and world nations.
* **India:** Physical features and states. Northern mountain region, rivers, Northern Great Plains, and Peninsular Plateau. Climate, natural vegetation, and agriculture. Minerals, industries, and energy sources. Road, water, rail, and air transport systems.
* **Kerala:** Physical features, districts, and characteristics. Rivers, climate, natural vegetation, and wildlife. Agriculture, research institutions, minerals, and industries. Energy sources and transport systems.

#### **Economics (5 Marks)**
* Indian economy and Five-Year Plans.
* Planning Commission and NITI Aayog.
* New economic reforms and financial institutions.
* Agricultural crops, minerals, and the Green Revolution.

#### **Indian Constitution (8 Marks)**
* Constituent Assembly, Preamble, and Citizenship.
* Fundamental Rights, Directive Principles, and Fundamental Duties.
* Organs of government.
* Major Constitutional Amendments: 42, 44, 52, 73, 74, 86, 91.
* Panchayati Raj and constitutional institutions/duties.
* Union List, State List
